What This Is

Agora exists to help any community stand up its own TV broadcasting station.

Not a streaming app

A real broadcast network on TV — living rooms, smart TVs, and the places your community already watches.

Your own station

A dedicated channel carrying your name, your programming, and your voice — not a folder in someone else's app.

Built to your reach

One network behind it all, so a single church, school, or civic group can broadcast at a scale that used to require a license and a tower.

How It Works

From idea to on-air, in three moves.

Tell us about your community

Church, school, sports program, or civic group — tell us who you serve and what you want your station to carry.

We build your station

Your channel is set up on the Agora Broadcasting Network — branded, scheduled, and ready for your content.

You go live

Your station broadcasts to smart TVs everywhere Agora reaches, with your community driving what airs.
