What This Is

Agora helps any community launch its own TV station.

Your own station

A dedicated station with your name, your programming, and your voice — not a folder in someone else's app.

On the screens your community watches

Smart TVs, streaming sticks, and phones — in living rooms, churches, schools, and senior facilities.

Built to scale

One network behind it all, so a single church, school, or civic group can run a station without building a studio or a transmitter.

How It Works

From idea to live, in three moves.

Tell us about your community

Church, school, sports program, or civic group — tell us who you serve and what you want your station to carry.

We build your station

Your station is set up on the Agora Broadcasting Network — branded, scheduled, and ready for your content.

You go live

Your station goes live on smart TVs, streaming sticks, and phones, with your community driving what airs.

Over the Air

Reach beyond the app.

Streaming comes first. Where a licensed low-power TV (LPTV) station is available, your programming can also go out over the air to antennas in your area, alongside the apps and smart TVs.

For communities

Over-the-air is an option in markets where Agora works with a licensed LPTV station. Availability depends on the station, its tower, and its coverage. Ask us what's possible near you.

Already have a license?

Plug your station into Agora's tools and programming feed to produce and distribute local content without a big production team.
